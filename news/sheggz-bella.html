"I'm Not a Gigolo": BBNaija's Sheggz Denies Living Off Ex-Girlfriend Bella

By Trending9ja Staff • October 6, 2026 • 3 min read

Photo: GistReel

BBNaija star Sheggz (Segun Olusemo) has denied claims that he lived off his ex-girlfriend Bella Okagbue. "I'm not a gigolo," he said, according to NaijaNews.

The two met on the 2022 season of Big Brother Naija. Sheggz said in August that their four-year relationship ended in January 2026. Bella accused him of owing her millions of naira and of making "hundreds of promises" that went unfulfilled.

Sheggz told Oyemykke that both partners contributed financially and that he never took money from her.
