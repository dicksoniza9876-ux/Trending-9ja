Toyosi Adesanya Opens Up on Five Surgeries While Trying to Conceive Before Welcoming Her Baby Girl

By Trending9ja Staff • October 12, 2026 • 2 min read

In an interview with Oyinmomo TV, actress Toyosi Adesanya said she had five surgeries in 2022 while trying to conceive, until her doctor advised her to stop. She credited Iyabo Ojo with supporting her and described difficulties in her former marriage.

She welcomed a baby girl with her husband in March 2026, according to Monday's Naija News roundup.
