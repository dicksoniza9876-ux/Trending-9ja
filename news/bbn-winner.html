Temi Nkem, 21, Wins Big Brother Naija Season 11 and Takes Home N160 Million

By Trending9ja Staff • October 5, 2026 • 3 min read

Photo: Ripples Nigeria

Temi Nkem, 21, has won Big Brother Naija Season 11, themed "Show Ya Sef." The result was announced during the live finale on Sunday night, hosted by Ebuka Obi-Uchendu.

The prize package is worth N160 million: N90 million in cash, a car worth N60 million and other sponsored rewards. Aikou was first runner-up and Sheba second runner-up. Temi Nkem received the most public votes, with voting closing on Friday, 2 October at 9 p.m.

The season began on 26 July with 24 housemates. Barry, Oyin, Ricky, Flora, Tram and Bluethopia were evicted on finale night. Teni and Oshamo performed at the finale.
