Oyebanji Sworn In for Second Term as Ekiti Governor

By Trending9ja Staff • October 17, 2026 • 3 min read

Ekiti State Governor Biodun Oyebanji was sworn in for a second term on Friday, becoming the first governor in the state’s history to succeed himself and serve two consecutive terms.

The ceremony was attended by President Tinubu, Senate President Akpabio, the Ooni of Ife and other governors. Oyebanji pledged to work harder and deliver more for the people of Ekiti.

Sources: New Telegraph · Independent
