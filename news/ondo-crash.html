Ondo Plane Crash: Epidemic Fears Mount as Bodies Remain Unrecovered

By Trending9ja Staff • October 14, 2026 • 3 min read

File image

Trending9ja'">

Fears of a possible epidemic are rising in Ondo State as recovery teams continue searching for victims of the recent plane crash, with several bodies still unrecovered.

The Punch reports that the state government has ordered river fumigation and the distribution of health kits to residents in affected communities. Senate, House of Representatives, and the Nigerian Air Force have launched probes into the circumstances of the crash.

Local authorities and health officials are working to prevent disease outbreaks while recovery operations proceed. The incident remains one of the major headlines dominating Nigerian newspapers today.
