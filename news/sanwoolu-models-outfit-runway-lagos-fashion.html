Sanwo-Olu Models Outfit on Runway at Lagos Fashion Week

By Trending9ja Staff • November 1, 2026 • 1 min read

Lagos State Governor Babajide Sanwo-Olu unexpectedly took to the runway at the 2026 Lagos Fashion Week, according to Vanguard. He joined professional models on stage to display a stylish ensemble during the fashion event.

The governor was dressed by Nigerian designer Ugo Monye in a regal off-white outfit. The clothing combined a traditional caftan with a kimono-themed design, which he paired with a beige fedora hat.

During the catwalk, Sanwo-Olu moved confidently across the stage while flashing peace signs and nodding to attendees. His appearance drew enthusiastic applause and cheering from the crowd in attendance.

In a statement concerning his participation, Sanwo-Olu mentioned that he briefly set aside his official executive duties to show support for Monye's talent. He emphasized that Lagos is steadily expanding as a center for fashion, culture, and creative endeavors.

The 2026 Lagos Fashion Week, carrying the theme "CRAFTED: Heritage," commenced on Wednesday at the Federal Palace Hotel in Lagos and is set to conclude on Sunday. The annual gathering brings designers, models, and fashion lovers together to celebrate African creative heritage.

Sources: Vanguard
