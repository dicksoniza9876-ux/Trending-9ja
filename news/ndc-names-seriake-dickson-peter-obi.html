NDC Names Seriake Dickson, Peter Obi to Lead 2027 Campaign Council

By Trending9ja Staff • October 27, 2026 • 1 min read

The Nigeria Democratic Congress has officially set up its campaign team ahead of the 2027 general elections, according to Vanguard. Senator Henry Seriake Dickson, who serves as the party's national leader, was appointed as the chairman of the National Campaign Council.

Vanguard reported that Peter Obi, the party's presidential flagbearer, will act as the co-chairman of the team. Additionally, the NDC national chairman and vice-presidential candidate, Senator Cleopas Moses Zuwoghe, was designated as vice-chairman alongside Barrister Ikenna Morgan Enekweizu as co-vice-chairman.

The position of director-general and council secretary was given to Mallam Ibrahim Hussain Abdulkarim. The political party stated that the newly established committee will oversee its election strategy across the country to garner votes for its development goals.

Sources: Vanguard
