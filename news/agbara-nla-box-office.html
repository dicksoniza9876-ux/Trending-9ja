Agbara Nla: The Return Opens With ₦170.59 Million, Biggest Non-December Nollywood Opening Ever

By Trending9ja Staff • October 5, 2026 • 3 min read

Photo: Nairametrics

Agbara Nla: The Return opened with ₦170.59 million from 28,468 admissions over 2 to 4 October, according to FilmX data reported by Nairametrics. Previews earned about ₦71.4 million, and the official weekend added ₦99.15 million from 16,223 admissions.

Sunday was the strongest day at ₦53.70 million from 8,678 admissions, after ₦15.38 million on Friday and ₦30.07 million on Saturday.

The records: biggest non-December opening for a Nollywood movie; third-biggest Nollywood opening on record, behind Everybody Loves Jenifa and Behind The Scenes; biggest October opening; and biggest opening for a faith-based film at the Nigerian box office, ahead of A Tribe Called Judah and Love in Every Word.

The film was released 33 years after the original, produced by Mount Zion Faith Ministries in the 1990s. Nairametrics says the result challenges December's dominance of Nollywood's biggest releases.

Sources: Nairametrics
