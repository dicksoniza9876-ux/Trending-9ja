Burna Boy Named Among Forbes Africa’s 15 Icons

By Trending9ja Staff • October 22, 2026 • 2 min read

Grammy-winning Nigerian singer Burna Boy has been named among Forbes Africa’s 15 African Icons in a special list celebrating the continent’s most influential figures.

He joins business leaders such as Aliko Dangote and WTO Director-General Ngozi Okonjo-Iweala. Forbes highlighted his role in projecting African music and culture to a global audience.

Burna Boy is also set to make his India debut at Rolling Loud India later this year.

Sources: The Nation • Forbes Africa • Blueprint
