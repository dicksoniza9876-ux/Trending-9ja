Football Roundup: Rohr Fumes Over Messi Snub, Leão Takes Ronaldo's No.7, Queiroz Exits Ghana

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 3 min read

Rohr vs security: Benin coach Gernot Rohr criticised security for stopping his players from taking photos with Lionel Messi and swapping shirts after Argentina won 3-0 in what was described as Messi's final international appearance.

Leão in Ronaldo's shirt: Rafael Leão will wear Cristiano Ronaldo's No.7 for Portugal's Nations League games against Denmark and Norway. Ronaldo reportedly left the camp after tensions with coach Jorge Jesus.

Queiroz out: Carlos Queiroz left Ghana after a 4-2 home defeat to The Gambia in AFCON 2027 qualifiers. The GFA said the contract was terminated by mutual agreement, ending a second spell of only two matches.

Osimhen watch: Galatasaray's doctor says Victor Osimhen has resumed some team training after missing three matches.
