EFCC Commences Hiring Drive Across Four Cadres, Outlines Application Criteria

By Trending9ja Staff • October 24, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Legit.ng

The Economic and Financial Crimes Commission has declared the start of a fresh hiring process for interested citizens across Nigeria. Applications are expected to be turned in solely through the agency's internet portal within seven days of its official launch, according to Legit.ng.

The recruitment covers four main cadres, ranging from entry-level positions to specialized roles for professionals. Requirements vary by category, with age limits spanning from eighteen to forty years depending on the position. Degree holders, diploma graduates, and secondary school certificate holders are eligible for specific roles.

All interested applicants must be Nigerian nationals with basic computer knowledge and certified medical fitness from a government hospital. The anti-graft body emphasized that candidates with tattoos or criminal records will be rejected, adding that no payment should be made to anyone as applying is completely free.
