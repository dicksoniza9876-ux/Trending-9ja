Atiku Demands Answers on NNPC's ₦11.2 Trillion Receivables as ADC Hits Back at Shettima

By Trending9ja Staff • October 5, 2026 • 3 min read

Atiku Abubakar, the ADC presidential candidate, has asked the government to explain how NNPC accounted for ₦11.2 trillion in receivables from the Federation. In a statement issued by Phrank Shaibu, he demanded details of pipeline security payments, contractors and project outcomes, and called for contracts, payment records and bidding processes to be published. He said NNPC's 2024 accounts recorded about ₦17.5 trillion in claims against the Federation.

The ADC also attacked Vice President Kashim Shettima. In a statement dated Sunday, 4 October, Director of Media Kola Ologbondiyan said Shettima's claim that Tinubu rescued Nigeria from economic collapse indicts the Buhari administration, which was also APC-led.

NDC chieftain Buba Galadima told the Naija Unfiltered Podcast that most Nigerians do not want Tinubu to return in 2027, and that letting the Southeast produce the president is the only way to unite the country.

On the labour front, NLC President Joe Ajaero said on the Mic On Podcast that the Tinubu government is disconnected from organised labour: "Everybody is their enemy."
