Depleted Super Eagles Hold Russia to Thrilling 3-3 Draw

By Trending9ja Staff • October 16, 2026 • 3 min read

Nigeria’s Super Eagles, fielding a heavily rotated and injury-hit squad, fought back twice to secure a 3-3 draw with Russia in an international friendly in Nizhny Novgorod.

Nine players made their senior debuts. Coach Eric Chelle praised the response after the 3-0 AFCON qualifying defeat to Guinea-Bissau and the absence of key players such as captain Wilfred Ndidi.

The result provides some encouragement ahead of crucial November qualifiers against Tanzania. Nigeria currently sits second in Group L.
