FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup: Nigeria to Face Venezuela in Quarter-Finals

By Trending9ja Staff • October 30, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Daily Post

Matchups for the quarter-final round of the ongoing FIFA Under-17 Women's World Cup in Morocco have been officially finalised. This comes after the completion of the Round of 16 games on Thursday, setting the stage for the next phase of the tournament starting Sunday.

Nigeria's national side progressed after securing a 2-1 victory over Mexico on Thursday night. In other round-of-16 matches, China triumphed over France, while Japan earned their spot among the final eight teams by overcoming Kenya with a 3-1 victory, according to Daily Post.

The upcoming quarter-final clashes will see Nigeria taking on Venezuela, while Ghana is scheduled to meet Japan. Furthermore, Brazil will play against China, and North Korea will battle the United States in their scheduled fixture on Sunday afternoon.
