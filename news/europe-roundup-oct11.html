Europe Weekend: Babiš's ANO Under Pressure in Czech Senate Vote, French Student Protests Spread, Lisbon Marches Over Living Costs

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 3 min read

Photo: Euronews / AP

Czech Republic: Prime Minister Andrej Babiš's ANO party faces a stiff test in the first round of the Senate election. Euronews notes that none of the five candidates who won outright came from ANO.

France: student protests over school conditions and teacher shortages could spread and affect the presidential race, Euronews reports, with similar protests in Belgium and Italy.

Portugal: demonstrators in Lisbon marched over high housing costs and low wages. Belgium: thousands urged the EU to act more decisively on climate change at a Brussels demonstration.

Sources: Euronews: Czech Senate · Euronews: French student protests · Euronews: Portugal
