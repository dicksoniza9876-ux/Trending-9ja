NAFDAC Destroys ₦2.6bn Fake Products in Rivers as Part of ₦5.3bn Week

By Trending9ja Staff • October 18, 2026 • 3 min read

NAFDAC has destroyed products worth ₦2.6 billion in Port Harcourt as part of a nationwide crackdown that saw more than ₦5.3 billion in fake and substandard goods destroyed in one week.

The items, including pharmaceuticals, chemicals, tomato paste and biscuits, were seized by the Nigeria Customs Service at Onne Port.

Sources: Channels Television
