Carrick Facing Sack at Manchester United Ahead of Bournemouth Clash

By Trending9ja Staff • October 25, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Daily Post

Manchester United head coach Michael Carrick is under intense pressure and could be dismissed if his side loses or draws against Bournemouth this Sunday. Reports from the UK Guardian indicate that the English manager has just one match remaining to salvage his position at the club.

The Red Devils have struggled recently, failing to secure a win across their previous seven matches in all contests. Their recent slump reached a low point on Wednesday when they suffered a heavy 4-1 beating against Como during a Champions League fixture.

Currently sitting 15th in the Premier League standings with seven points, United are already considering potential successors. High-profile coaches such as Julian Nagelsmann, Antonio Conte, and Eddie Howe have been linked to the managerial seat, following Carrick’s permanent appointment earlier this summer.
