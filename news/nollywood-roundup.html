Nollywood Roundup: Tonto Dikeh Builds a Church, Joke Silva Turns 65, Julius Agwu Opens Up

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 3 min read

Tonto Dikeh: Ripples Nigeria reports that the actress has begun construction of a church in Abuja, following her August announcement that she was launching her own church.

Joke Silva at 65: The Actors Guild, Richard Mofe-Damijo and fans celebrated the veteran actress as she turned 65.

Julius Agwu: The veteran comedian and singer says in an emotional video that friends and colleagues deserted him during his health crisis.

Judy Austin vs Rita Edochie: Earlier this year, Ripples reported that actress Judy Austin took Rita Edochie to a High Court in Abuja over alleged defamation, with the headline stating a demand of N15 billion and N50 million.
