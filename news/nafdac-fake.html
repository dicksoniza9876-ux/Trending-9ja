NAFDAC Destroys ₦5.3 Billion Fake Products in One Week

By Trending9ja Staff • October 17, 2026 • 3 min read

The National Agency for Food and Drug Administration and Control (NAFDAC) has destroyed fake products valued at ₦5.3 billion in a single week as part of an intensified crackdown.

The action comes amid growing public concern over the proliferation of fake drugs, drinks and foods across the country. Health authorities continue to urge citizens to buy only from verified sources.

Sources: Punch (Saturday newspaper review)
