Julian Alvarez Scores Only Goal As Atletico Madrid Beat Deportivo

By Trending9ja Staff • October 24, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Vanguard

Julian Alvarez delivered a key performance for Atletico Madrid, securing a narrow 1-0 victory against Deportivo La Coruna in La Liga on Saturday. His crucial strike reduced the point gap between Atletico and league leaders Barcelona to just two points, according to Vanguard.

The Argentine attacker found the net early in the second half after connecting with a low cross sent by Alejandro Grimaldo. Alvarez had previously struggled with fitness issues and faced criticism from supporters earlier in the season following his failed transfer attempt during the summer window.

Deportivo created several dangerous moments, but goalkeeper Jan Oblak made vital saves to preserve the lead, including a late stop against Kevin Sanchez. Meanwhile, Villarreal earned a late 2-1 victory over Valencia in a separate Spanish league match where both teams had players dismissed.

Sources: Vanguard
