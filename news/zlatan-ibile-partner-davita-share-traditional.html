Zlatan Ibile And Partner Davita Share Traditional Wedding Pictures

By Trending9ja Staff • October 24, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Punch

Nigerian afrobeat performer Omoniyi Raphael, widely recognized as Zlatan, has shared stunning photos with his partner, Davita Lamai, ahead of their traditional marriage event. According to Punch, the music star displayed the traditional photoshoot images on his verified Instagram handle on Thursday.

The musician accompanied the pictures with a caption stating that it was time for them to tie the knot. Punch reported that fans alongside famous personalities, including content creator Enioluwa Adeoluwa and comedian Broda Shaggi, sent warm congratulations to the couple in the comment section.

This development comes after the pair officially revealed their marital plans in a short video clip published online earlier this month. The report noted that the couple had initially made the announcement on October 8 before holding their traditional ceremony.
