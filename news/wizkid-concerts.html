Wizkid Confirms Abuja and Lagos Headline Shows for Detty December 2026

By Trending9ja Staff • October 13, 2026 • 3 min read

Afrobeats superstar Wizkid has locked in two major Nigerian headline dates for the 2026 Detty December season.

He is billed to headline the Live Out Loud Music Festival at Eagle Square, Abuja, on Sunday, 13 December 2026. Five days later, on Friday, 18 December, he will open the Detty December Fest in Lagos.

The announcements mark his first headline stage appearances in Nigeria this year and add to an already stacked festive calendar that includes multi-artist bills featuring Burna Boy, Rema, Davido, Ayra Starr, Asake, Tems and others at venues such as the Moshood Abiola National Stadium.

Early-bird tickets for the Abuja show have sold out, with organisers directing fans to official ticketing platforms. Fans are already calling it one of the must-see nights of the year.
