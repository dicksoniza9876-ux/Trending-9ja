Rangers Exit CAF Champions League After 3-0 Defeat To MC Alger

By Trending9ja Staff • October 23, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Daily Post

Nigerian club Rangers International have been knocked out of the CAF Champions League following a 3-0 loss to MC Alger in Algeria. The result allowed the hosts to reverse a two-goal first-leg deficit and advance to the group stage with a 3-2 aggregate triumph.

During the match, Rangers goalkeeper Destiny Abuchi performed well initially, denying the Algerian team several scoring opportunities in the opening half. However, Chahreddine Boukholda eventually broke through for MC Alger, scoring the game's opening goal just two minutes prior to the hour mark.

Abuchi later stopped Mohamed Bangoura’s penalty kick in the 70th minute to protect Rangers' overall lead. Bangoura nevertheless scored six minutes before full-time, and Soufiane Bayazid netted during injury time to complete the dramatic comeback victory for MC Alger.
