Venezuela Coach Frías Reacts to U-17 World Cup Defeat Against Nigeria

By Trending9ja Staff • November 2, 2026 • 1 min read

Venezuela head coach Dayana Frías has reflected on her team's 1-0 loss to Nigeria's Flamingos in the quarter-finals of the 2026 FIFA U-17 Women's World Cup, according to reports by Daily Post and Complete Sports.

The South American squad was eliminated from the tournament after yielding a late penalty. Throughout much of the contest, Venezuela's defense maintained a solid structure despite strong attacking pressure from the Nigerian side, keeping the fixture goalless deep into the match.

The crucial moment arrived when Venezuelan defender Migleydys Bolsegui was penalised for a foul on Oluebube Umejiaku inside the box. Azeezat Oduntan stepped up to score the resulting spot kick, sealing the win for Nigeria. Complete Sports noted that the victory propels the Flamingos into a semi-final fixture against Brazil.

Following the match, Frías told FIFA that her team performed admirably overall but suffered the consequences of one costly error. She added that the competition served as a valuable learning process and pointed out that seven of her current players will remain eligible to compete in the tournament next year.

Sources: Daily Post · Complete Sports
