Ex-Minister Uche Nnaji Drops Degree Claim, Lists WAEC Result in INEC Filing and Promises Traders Free Shops

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 3 min read

Uche Nnaji, who resigned as science and technology minister in October 2025 over certificate allegations, has dropped a degree claim in his INEC filing and listed a WAEC result instead, Premium Times reports in Monday's newspaper reviews. He faces a forgery trial.

Daily Trust adds that a former minister who resigned over a certificate scandal was included on INEC's final list after submitting a WAEC result. The Guardian reports that Nnaji has promised free shops to traders displaced by market demolitions as part of his campaign.

These details come from a roundup of the papers, not the original articles.

Sources: FellowPress newspaper headlines, 12 Oct 2026
