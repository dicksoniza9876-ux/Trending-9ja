Felabration 2026 Heads to Grand Finale Tomorrow – Fearless Powers Rap & Dance Challenges

By Trending9ja Staff • October 17, 2026 • 3 min read

The week-long Felabration 2026 at the New Afrika Shrine in Ikeja continues with strong live energy and heads toward its grand finale on Sunday, 18 October.

Fearless Energy Drink has returned as a sponsor, powering rap and dance challenges that give emerging talent a platform. Headliners include Femi Kuti & The Positive Force, Made Kuti, and Seun Kuti & Egypt 80.

The theme remains “Who Are You?” — pure offline Afrobeat culture.

Sources: New Telegraph · Voice of Nigeria
