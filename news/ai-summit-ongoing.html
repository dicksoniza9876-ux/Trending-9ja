AI in Health Summit Underway in Abuja (19–20 Oct)

By Trending9ja Staff • October 19, 2026 • 3 min read

Image: Voice of Nigeria

The Artificial Intelligence in Health Summit is taking place in Abuja on 19–20 October, bringing policymakers, innovators and health experts together to develop practical frameworks for responsible AI use in Nigerian healthcare.

Discussions cover governance, data, workforce gaps, and locally developed tools. Speakers include representatives from Africa CDC, Smart Africa and Nigerian institutions. A related ministerial longtable is also scheduled.

Sources: Voice of Nigeria • Science Nigeria • AHFID
