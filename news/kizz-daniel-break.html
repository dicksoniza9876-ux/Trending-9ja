Kizz Daniel: "I Paused for My Mom, Not an Album". Fans Worry After Lagos Concert Appearance

By Trending9ja Staff • October 11, 2026 • 3 min read

Photo: Ripples Nigeria (file photo)

Afrobeats singer Kizz Daniel has explained why he stepped back from music this year. Replying to a fan on X who guessed he was working on a new album, he wrote: "I paused for my Mom, not an album." He attributes the break to the death of his mother earlier this year.

Legit.ng also reported that fans expressed concern over his appearance at a Lagos concert, with the headline saying he looked "unrecognisable."

Kizz Daniel has not announced a new project. Fans have been sending messages of support.
