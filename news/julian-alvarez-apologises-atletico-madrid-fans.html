Julian Alvarez Apologises To Atletico Madrid Fans Over Summer Transfer Saga

By Trending9ja Staff • October 25, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Vanguard

Atletico Madrid striker Julian Alvarez has offered an apology to the club's supporters following his attempt to force a transfer away during the summer break. The Argentine international admitted that his previous comments about leaving were inappropriate in timing and delivery, according to Vanguard.

The former Manchester City forward had been heavily linked with a move to Barcelona, leading to frustration among Atletico supporters who booed him early in the season. Alvarez stated he fully understood the fans' anger and expressed gratitude to his teammates and fans for their ongoing backing.

The 26-year-old player has recently shown improvements on the pitch, scoring crucial goals against Stuttgart in the Champions League and netting the winner against Deportivo La Coruna. Alvarez noted that he is regaining full fitness and confidence as he works to rebuild his relationship with the supporters.
