FWIFT Nigeria Set to Host 2026 National Convention in Lagos

By Trending9ja Staff • October 23, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: BellaNaija

FWIFT Nigeria has announced plans to hold its upcoming national convention in Lagos on October 29, 2026. According to BellaNaija, the event will gather media professionals at Glover Memorial Hall under the theme, "One Industry, Many Voices: We Build Together."

The gathering aims to bring together female practitioners in radio, television, film, advertising, and digital platforms. Organisers stated that art curator Mrs. Sandra Mbanefo Obiago is scheduled to give the keynote speech, alongside panel sessions featuring prominent figures like Kadaria Ahmed, Biodun Stephens, and Gbemi Olateru-Olagbegi.

FWIFT explained that the program will feature speed networking, interactive panels, and fireside chats on global media careers. The group noted that its primary goal is to empower female creators through mentorship, skill training, and strategic industry connections to elevate African storytelling worldwide.
