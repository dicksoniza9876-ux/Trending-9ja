Tinubu Celebrates BusinessDay Publisher Frank Aigbogun on 70th Birthday

By Trending9ja Staff • October 31, 2026 • 1 min read

Photo: Punch

President Bola Tinubu has extended his warm congratulations to Frank Aigbogun, the publisher of BusinessDay newspaper, as he prepares to celebrate his 70th birthday. According to Punch, the Nigerian leader acknowledged the veteran journalist's significant role in shaping public discussion through insightful economic reporting.

In a personal statement released on Saturday, the president highlighted Aigbogun's dedication to credible business reporting and ethical media practices. Tinubu noted that the publisher's work has provided essential analysis on investment opportunities, economic policies, and national developments since he established the media organization back in 2001.

Furthermore, the president urged experienced media figures to continue supporting the country's institutional progress, particularly during this period of ongoing economic reforms. Tinubu concluded his message by wishing the media entrepreneur continued good health, renewed strength, and further achievements in the years ahead.
