Agbara Nla Grosses ₦307m, Ranks Fourth in 2026 Nollywood

By Trending9ja Staff • October 22, 2026 • 2 min read

Nollywood faith-based film Agbara Nla: The Return has grossed over ₦307.2 million at the box office, according to its distributors.

The movie, a remake of the 1993 classic by Mike Bamiloye and directed by his sons, is now the highest-grossing Nollywood title ever for an October release and the fourth highest-grossing Nollywood film of 2026.

Distributors Nile Group thanked audiences for packed cinemas and sold-out shows.

Sources: Punch • Telegraph Nigeria • Nile Group
