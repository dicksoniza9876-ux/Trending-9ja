Felabration 2026 Grand Finale Today at the New Afrika Shrine

By Trending9ja Staff • October 18, 2026 • 3 min read

The week-long Felabration 2026 reaches its grand finale today, Sunday 18 October, at the New Afrika Shrine in Ikeja. The theme remains “Who Are You?”

Live performances continue with Femi Kuti & The Positive Force, Made Kuti, Seun Kuti & Egypt 80 and other acts. Fearless Energy Drink has powered rap and dance challenges throughout the week. Pure offline Afrobeat energy to close the festival.

Sources: New Telegraph · Voice of Nigeria
