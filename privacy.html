Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trending9ja ("we", "us") collects, uses and protects information when you visit our website. We handle personal data in line with the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 and other laws that apply to our visitors.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us, such as your name and email address if you contact us.
  • Usage information collected automatically, such as pages viewed, device type, browser and approximate location, through cookies and similar technologies.

Cookies and advertising

We use cookies to run the website and remember your choices. We also use Google AdSense to show ads. Google and its partners may use cookies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites.

  • You can learn how Google uses data from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com, or visit aboutads.info.
  • You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. Some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

How we use information

  • To operate, secure and improve the website.
  • To respond to messages you send us.
  • To show advertising that helps fund the site.

Third-party links and advertisers

Our stories link to the websites of the publishers we cite, and our ads may link to advertiser websites. We do not control those sites and are not responsible for their privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to complain to a data protection authority such as the Nigeria Data Protection Commission. Contact us using the details on our Contact page to make a request.

Children

Our site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to count visits and see which stories are read. It uses cookies and collects anonymous usage data such as pages viewed, approximate location and device type. You can block it with your browser settings or an ad blocker.
